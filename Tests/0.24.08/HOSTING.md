# Moon Palace 0.24.08 test page

R7 packages presentation media in Data/Presentation/selection.mpui (27,908,715 bytes / 394 entries), independently of Battle and character Sound directories. The worker verifies the pack hash and caches it locally. Selection readiness includes all five portrait libraries (all actions and voice blobs), selection artwork/3D scene, ACS, declaration and report assets. Pack-byte and decode completion drive pre-selection progress. First-time character changes and ACS entry use prepared assets. Battle Loading separately tracks initial engine readiness, selected character metadata/atlas image loads, declaration readiness, stage download/decompression/parse and GPU preparation; 100% is set only when starting battle and the loading view closes immediately.

Main-menu highlight is a white text stroke and outer text glow (no item border), sparks originate at the selected item's center. Distant upper entries are lighter and lower entries have 1.5× opacity. Battle-only HUD controls render above the exterior pattern and are hidden on menu/presentation pages. Standard lethal hurt-state entry cannot retain an interrupted attack animation during hitshake. Timelines/*.air and Tools/quote-timelines.py provide editable AIR-style exports and an importer. Edits require rebuilding the pack and refreshing its worker checksum/size/path table; the AIR files are source documents, not automatically read during gameplay.

R6 halves the current cursor-spark drift again to 15.4px/s. A cached white alpha mask retains the original spark shape and transparency while removing blue. The selected item has a transparent interior, white rounded border and outer glow; the former filled halo is removed. Particle count, menu position, RESULT and KO logic are unchanged.

R5 suppresses buffered commands outside active combat while retaining the current state's source control flag. Once controllable, crouch releases through S12 to S0 and walking releases to S0, matching ordinary locomotion; attacks and airborne states keep their own completion chains. The page pattern now uses four exterior rectangles instead of a full-screen even-odd clip, so it cannot paint over RESULT if clipping fails. Result entry cancels any entrance/loading cover and exposes preparation errors through the loading panel. The reported blank RESULT was not reproduced locally; actual match-end rendering was checked at the reported portrait viewport. Main-menu center moves down 90 logical pixels, selected item gains a white halo, sparks use 30.8px/s (half) and 8 particles per side (20% fewer).

R4 adds a circular main menu: the current item and its two neighbors remain clear, with more distant items fading at the edges. Arrow keys animate the list; pointer dragging scrolls it and only a short stationary tap activates an item. The selected item emits the Mercurius Sudoku eight-point cursor spark (lossless WebP), with its original short lifetime, drift and rotation directed left and right. Menu navigation uses the compressed btn-select Ogg. Report Card uses the updated transparent pattern over #E7C7FF. Projectile hits accepted by a helper's HitBy/HitOverride now consume the projectile contact as well, so Moon Tiara and Saturn Press Crusher both finish their collision animations. No Moon LOSE/TLose timelines changed.

R3 adds animated selection, ACS, intro and result portraits for Moon, Jupiter, Neptune and Saturn. Open portrait-review.html to replay each presentation without a match. Images are cropped WebP with a logical 1280×720 canvas; voices are compressed Ogg Vorbis. Only the selected characters' declaration assets are prepared before battle. Selection and ACS share the same animation clock on handoff.

Moon and Jupiter complete their silent victory pose transition before speaking. Moon defeat loops the crying animation without lip sync. Time-over defeat uses TLose; Moon TLose 02a and 02b play sequentially. Other speaking sections use three mouth shapes driven by voice loudness, rather than phoneme recognition. Data/Quote/*.json contains editable 60 Hz timelines. Tools/build-quote-portraits.py rebuilds the compressed assets from the private Quote folders, with Pillow, NumPy and SoundFile installed. Original PNG and WAV files are not included.

R2 removes the old result-state watchdog entirely: no animation-loop rescue may force S0 and bypass source cleanup. Jupiter's 3005→3006→3007→0 chain removes E9066 under KO and time-over for both players. Supplemental WebP sprite sheets for Jupiter, Neptune and Saturn supply native SFF sprites referenced by Moon's foreign A805; they do not replace its AIR elements, timing or collision table. The sprite supplements are loaded with the selected characters. B1-9-CHECK.md records the five-character usage audit; none of the nine generic changes were imported.
